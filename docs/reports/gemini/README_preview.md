## ⚡ 2. Architectural Pillars & Core Capabilities / 核心架构与系统机制

### 📅 A. Multi-Period Reporting Window Switcher / 动态时序报告期切换
Unlike static PDF reports, our dashboard is architected around a reactive time-series state engine. Users can toggle instantly between six comprehensive reporting periods:
- **2026 Q3 (Current Latest Horizon)**: Full third-quarter peak-surge data, ILA labor hedges, and EU ETS Phase II deductions.
- **2026 H1 (Semi-Annual Baseline)**: First-half consolidated performance metrics and early nearshoring trends.
- **2026 Q1 & 2026 Q2**: Granular quarterly performance breakdowns and seasonal freight surge modeling.
- **2025 H2 & 2025 FY**: Historical benchmarking and year-over-year (YoY) inflation contrast analysis.
* **Instant Synchronicity**: Switching windows automatically updates WCI rate benchmarks, Supply Chain Stress Indices (SCSI), Recharts trendlines, and Gemini RAG grounding context.

### 🛰️ B. Real-Time Telemetry MUX Ticker / 4秒实时高频运价与压力指数流
Engineered for trading-desk responsiveness, the top telemetry ticker streams live industry pulse data every 4 seconds:
- **World Container Index (WCI) Spot Rates**: Transpacific, Asia–Europe, and transatlantic benchmark rate indicators.
- **Global Supply Chain Pressure Index (SCSI)**: Quantitative index synthesizing port dwell hours, container turnaround times, and fuel indices.
- **Interactive Mechanical Controls**: Operators can Pause, Resume, or Force Sync (Pull) telemetry polling on demand.

### 🤖 C. Grounded Gemini 3.8 RAG Intelligence / 联网检索增强问答引擎
Equipped with server-side Google GenAI SDK and real-time Search Grounding:
- **Zero Hallucination Guarantee**: Every AI output is verified against live Google Search web citations and institutional logistics indices.
- **Pre-Configured 2026 Q3 Strategic Chips**: Instant single-click prompts for US East Coast ILA negotiations, EU ETS Phase II carbon levies, Nearshoring 2.0 origin audits, and autonomous agentic booking APIs.
