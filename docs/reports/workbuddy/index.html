核心判断 · 中国视角

Core Assessment · China View

2026年前三季度，全球供应链在霍尔木兹海峡危机、中美贸易摩擦升级与人工智能需求爆发三重力量交织下持续承压与重构。中国外贸前三季度达39.94万亿元、同比增长18.8%，创近5年同期最快增速，出口、进口双双实现两位数增长；全球供应链压力指数（GSCPI）从年初的0.45升至4月的1.84，7月一度回落至0.79的四个月低点，随后因海峡局势反复于9月再度回升至1.28。航运呈现"苏伊士回血、霍尔木兹紧绷"的分化格局：班轮公司自9月起逐步重返红海—苏伊士航线，绕行好望角运力份额降至4.6%的历史低位，但霍尔木兹海峡通行量仍不足正常水平一半。Agentic AI驱动的电子元件贸易成为全球贸易最大增量引擎，全球供应链正从"效率优先"向"韧性为王、智能驱动"的新范式加速跃迁。

In the first three quarters of 2026, global supply chains remained under pressure and restructuring amid the intertwined forces of the Strait of Hormuz crisis, escalating US-China trade tensions, and surging AI-driven demand. China's foreign trade reached 39.94 trillion yuan in 9M 2026 (+18.8% YoY), the fastest growth in nearly five years, with double-digit growth in both exports and imports. The GSCPI surged from 0.45 at the start of the year to 1.84 in April, eased to a four-month low of 0.79 in July, then rebounded to 1.28 in September. Shipping showed a bifurcated pattern — "Suez recovering, Hormuz still tight": carriers began returning to the Red Sea–Suez route in September, cutting Cape of Good Hope detours to a two-year low of 4.6% of capacity, while Hormuz transits remained below half of normal. Agentic-AI-driven electronics trade became the single largest growth engine for global trade, accelerating the shift from "efficiency-first" to a new paradigm of "resilience reigns, intelligence drives."

Core Assessment · Global View

2026年前三季度，全球贸易顶住地缘政治压力稳步扩张：WTO货物贸易晴雨表升至102.0，电子元件分项达104.9创阶段新高，AI相关贸易成为最大增量引擎，出口订单指数升至103.5预示动能延续。全球供应链压力指数（GSCPI）自4月峰值1.84回落后，因霍尔木兹局势反复于9月回升至1.28。中东航运呈"两极分化"：班轮公司自9月起重返红海—苏伊士航线，绕行好望角运力份额降至4.6%的两年新低，苏伊士集装箱净吨位前8个月同比增长54.2%；而霍尔木兹通行量仍不足正常水平一半，战争险费率重回两位数。跨太平洋航线运价高位运行，全球供应链正加速向"韧性为王、智能驱动"的新范式跃迁。

Global trade expanded steadily through 9M 2026 despite geopolitical pressure: the WTO Goods Trade Barometer rose to 102.0, with the electronic-components sub-index at a stage high of 104.9 and export orders at 103.5 — AI-related trade is the largest incremental engine. The GSCPI eased from its April peak of 1.84 but rebounded to 1.28 in September amid renewed Hormuz tensions. Middle East shipping bifurcated: carriers returned to Red Sea–Suez routes from September, cutting Cape of Good Hope detours to a two-year-low 4.6% of capacity with Suez container net tonnage up 54.2% YoY in Jan–Aug, while Hormuz transits stayed below half of normal and war-risk premiums returned to double digits. Transpacific rates remain elevated as global supply chains accelerate toward a new paradigm of resilience and intelligence.

全球供应链管理市场规模

Global SCM Market Size

2026年全球供应链管理（SCM）市场规模预计达到约380亿美元（360iResearch），较2025年的354亿美元同比增长约7.4%。到2032年，该市场预计将增长至596亿美元，CAGR为7.72%。云部署解决方案占比超过75%，AI驱动的预测分析和自动化正成为核心增长引擎。

The global Supply Chain Management (SCM) market is estimated to reach approximately $38 billion in 2026 (360iResearch), up 7.4% from $35.4 billion in 2025. The market is projected to reach $59.6 billion by 2032 at a CAGR of 7.72%. Cloud-based solutions account for over 75% of deployments, with AI-driven predictive analytics and automation serving as core growth engines.

全球贸易总量

Global Trade Volume

WTO数据显示，2026年三季度货物贸易晴雨表指数升至102.0（6月为101.7），连续高于100基准线，全球贸易动能持续增强。电子元件分项高达104.9，反映AI投资对贸易的强支撑；出口订单指数升至103.5，预示未来数月贸易仍将扩张。AI相关电子元件贸易延续高增长，成为抵消中东冲突负面影响的关键力量。

WTO data shows the Goods Trade Barometer rose to 102.0 in Q3 2026 (up from 101.7 in June), holding above the 100 baseline as global trade momentum strengthens. The electronic components component hit 104.9, reflecting strong AI-investment support for trade; the export orders index climbed to 103.5, signaling continued expansion in coming months. AI-related electronics trade keeps surging, acting as a key counterweight to Middle East conflict disruptions.

中国外贸：历史性突破

2026年前三季度，中国货物贸易进出口总值达39.94万亿元，同比增长18.8%，创近5年同期最快增速。其中出口23.2万亿元、增长16.4%，进口16.74万亿元、增长22.3%，进口增速连续7个月超两成。季度增速逐季加快：Q1 +15.3%、Q2 +18.3%、Q3 +22.3%。集成电路出口增长99.1%，电动汽车、锂电池、风电机组分别增长72.3%、36.4%、22.4%。

中国物流运行：稳中有进

前三季度全国社会物流总额263.2万亿元，同比增长5.4%，与GDP增速保持同步。工业品物流总额同比增长5.6%，对增长贡献率达81%。单位与居民物品物流总额增长6.6%，其中三季度增长8.0%、较二季度加快1.8个百分点。新能源汽车、动力电池、太阳能电池生产物流量分别增长29.7%、46.9%、14.0%。物流业景气指数连续5个月处于扩张区间，9月达51.5%。

全球贸易韧性

Global Trade Resilience

尽管经历霍尔木兹海峡危机，全球商品贸易仍展现出显著韧性，三季度WTO货物贸易晴雨表升至102.0。WTO数据显示，2025年AI赋能商品贸易大增21.9%至4.18万亿美元，贡献全球商品贸易增量的约42%，强势动能延续至2026年。亚洲仍是全球AI供应链中心，占全球AI商品贸易约62%。

Despite the Strait of Hormuz crisis, global merchandise trade showed remarkable resilience, with the WTO Goods Trade Barometer rising to 102.0 in Q3. AI-enabling goods trade surged 21.9% to $4.18 trillion in 2025 (WTO), generating ~42% of total merchandise trade growth, and momentum continued into 2026. Asia remains the center of the AI supply chain, accounting for ~62% of global AI-goods trade.

区域分化显著

Regional Divergence

2026年各地区贸易增长明显分化：亚洲受AI电子需求驱动领跑，北美成为增长最快的AI硬件市场，中东受霍尔木兹危机冲击最深（三季度苏伊士方向开始恢复）。WTO出口订单指数（103.5）与空运指数（102.8）均指向贸易前景趋稳，但集装箱运输分项（99.6）仍低于趋势水平。

Trade growth diverged sharply across regions in 2026. Asia led with AI-driven electronics demand, while North America emerged as the fastest-growing AI-hardware market. The Middle East remained hardest hit by the Hormuz crisis, though Suez transit began recovering from Q3. The WTO's export-orders index (103.5) and air-freight index (102.8) both point to firmer trade ahead, even as container shipping dipped below trend (99.6).

行业动态 Industry Dynamics

核心事件：中东航运"两极分化"——苏伊士回血、霍尔木兹紧绷

Key Event: Middle East Shipping Bifurcation — Suez Recovers, Hormuz Stays Tight

2月28日美以对伊朗发动军事打击以来，霍尔木兹海峡——承载全球约1/4海运石油贸易和1/5液化天然气贸易的关键咽喉——通航量断崖式下跌。进入三季度，中东航运出现显著"两极分化"：红海—苏伊士方向逐步恢复，马士基、MSC、达飞、中远海运等自9月起陆续重返苏伊士航线，绕行好望角运力份额降至4.6%的两年新低，前8个月苏伊士运河集装箱净吨位同比增长54.2%；而波斯湾—霍尔木兹方向依然高度紧张，9月通行商船骤降至日均约10艘、较5月以来平均水平腰斩，战争险费率重回两位数。苏伊士的恢复并不等于中东风险整体下降——航运风险正从"区域风险"细化为"航线级风险"。

Since the US-Israel strikes on Iran on February 28, the Strait of Hormuz — carrying ~1/4 of global seaborne oil and 1/5 of LNG trade — saw transit volumes collapse. In Q3, Middle East shipping bifurcated sharply: the Red Sea–Suez corridor gradually recovered as Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM, and COSCO returned to Suez routes from September, cutting Cape of Good Hope detours to a two-year low of 4.6% of capacity, with Suez container net tonnage up 54.2% YoY in the first eight months; meanwhile the Persian Gulf–Hormuz corridor stayed highly tense, with daily transits falling to ~10 vessels in September (half the post-May average) and war-risk premiums back to double digits. Suez's recovery does not mean overall Middle East risk has fallen — shipping risk is fragmenting from "regional" to "route-level."

主要贸易政策变动

Major Trade Policy Changes

  • 美国最高法院关税裁决（1月）：限制总统单方面大幅调整关税权力。Kearney报告指出平均实际关税率已上升30%。 US Supreme Court Tariff Ruling (Jan): Limits presidential power to unilaterally adjust tariffs. Kearney reports applied tariff rates have risen 30% on average.
  • 中国稀土出口管制升级（10月9日）：对全部中重稀土及26类稀土加工设备、原材料实施出口许可，11月8日落地，直接冲击半导体与军工供应链。 China Rare-Earth Export Controls (Oct 9): Export licensing imposed on all medium/heavy rare earths plus 26 categories of processing equipment and materials, effective Nov 8 — directly hitting semiconductor and defense supply chains.
  • 美国加征100%关税威胁（10月10日）：拟对华商品加征100%关税（11月1日生效），叠加此前半导体出口限制，中美贸易摩擦显著升温；随后中美高层重启磋商。 US 100% Tariff Threat (Oct 10): Additional 100% tariff on Chinese goods proposed (effective Nov 1), on top of semiconductor export restrictions — US-China tensions rising sharply; high-level talks subsequently resumed.
  • USMCA续期受阻（6月）：美国拒绝以现有形式续期16年，改为年度审查机制。60%受调查企业尚未采取应对行动。 USMCA Renewal Blocked (Jun): US declines 16-year renewal; shifts to annual reviews. 60% of surveyed firms have taken no preparatory action.
  • 欧盟关税豁免取消（7月生效）：取消150欧元以下包裹关税豁免，每件加征3欧元，直接影响跨境电商。 EU De Minimis Removal (effective July): Eliminates duty exemption for packages under €150; €3 surcharge per item, directly impacting cross-border e-commerce.

热门议题 Hot Topics

Agentic AI：供应链的新"数字员工"

Agentic AI: Supply Chain's New "Digital Workforce"

2026年被业界称为供应链AI的"iPhone时刻"。Agentic AI（代理型人工智能）从概念验证走向规模化商用，AI从"回答问题"进化到"自主执行"。Gartner预测，到2026年底40%的企业应用将集成专属AI代理。壹沓科技、全球捷运等企业已在物流截单、合同审核、运价解析等场景实现5倍效率提升。青岛港自研"星港大模型"实现安全隐患全闭环管控，监管覆盖率提升至98%以上。全球港航信息化市场规模达6,406亿元，+7.45%。

2026 is called the "iPhone moment" for supply chain AI. Agentic AI has moved from proof-of-concept to commercial deployment, evolving from "answering questions" to "autonomous execution." Gartner predicts 40% of enterprise applications will integrate dedicated AI agents by end-2026. Companies like Yita Technology are delivering 5x efficiency improvements in document processing, contract review, and rate parsing. Port of Qingdao's AI model achieves 98%+ safety coverage. Global port & shipping IT market reaches RMB 640.6 billion, +7.45%.

供应链区域化：从"去风险"到"再布局"

Supply Chain Regionalization: From De-risking to Re-design

64%的制造商已推进或正在推进生产区域化（Alpega调查）。中国58%的供应链高管计划增加供应商数量，43%预期贸易壁垒将进一步增加。北美买家的采购集中度显著下降，印度、越南等市场承接了大量订单转移。Prologis调查显示：66%的企业计划扩大在墨西哥的布局，62%计划扩大在美国的布局。然而，近岸/回岸仍占总体采购的较小份额——成本结构、品类适配和供应商深度仍是主要制约。

64% of manufacturers have regionalized or are regionalizing production (Alpega survey). 58% of Chinese supply chain executives plan to increase supplier counts; 43% expect further trade barrier increases. North American buyers' sourcing concentration has materially decreased, with India, Vietnam absorbing significant order shifts. Prologis survey: 66% plan to expand in Mexico, 62% in the US. However, nearshoring/reshoring remains a small share of total sourcing — cost structures, category fit, and supplier depth remain key constraints.

能源与航运：霍尔木兹的长尾效应

Energy & Shipping: Hormuz Long-Tail Effects

霍尔木兹海峡危机虽在6月因停火协议出现转机，但其影响远未结束——三季度中东航运呈现"苏伊士回血、霍尔木兹紧绷"的分化。红海—苏伊士方向逐步恢复，班轮公司自9月起重返，绕行好望角运力份额降至4.6%的两年新低；但波斯湾—霍尔木兹方向通行量仍不足正常水平一半，战争险费率重回两位数。能源贸易的物理路线和合同期限仍在结构性改变：主要进口方加速采购来源多元化，增加长期协议和战略储备。中欧(亚)班列前8月开行24,605列、增长9.7%，陆路通道战略价值凸显。

The Hormuz crisis, though eased by the June ceasefire, has lasting effects — Q3 saw Middle East shipping bifurcate into "Suez recovering, Hormuz tight." The Red Sea–Suez corridor gradually recovered as carriers returned from September, cutting Cape of Good Hope detours to a two-year low of 4.6% of capacity; but the Persian Gulf–Hormuz corridor stayed below half of normal transit, with war-risk premiums back in double digits. Energy trade routes and contract structures keep restructuring: major importers accelerate source diversification, longer-term agreements, and strategic reserves. China-Europe (Asia) Railway Express ran 24,605 trains in Jan-Aug (+9.7%), underscoring the strategic value of land corridors.

供应链韧性指数：从"事后救火"到"事前预判"

Supply Chain Resilience: From Reactive to Predictive

第四届链博会发布的《全球供应链促进报告2026》揭示了关键洞察：2018-2025年间，全球供应链促进指数年均增长16.07%，创新指数增长14.4%，而韧性指数仅增长3.23%。韧性指数已处于体系"枢纽"地位，与促进、连接、创新之间存在显著因果关联。采用AI自主决策系统的企业响应中断速度提升62%、恢复速度加快60%（Accenture）。"韧性为王"正在成为新的行业共识。

The 4th CISCE released the Global Supply Chain Promotion Report 2026, revealing that from 2018-2025, the promotion index grew at 16.07% annually, innovation at 14.4%, but resilience at just 3.23% — now in a pivotal "hub" position. Companies adopting autonomous AI-driven supply chains respond to disruptions 62% faster and recover 60% quicker (Accenture). "Resilience reigns" is becoming the new industry consensus.

趋势一：AI从工具到决策主体

Trend 1: AI from Tool to Decision-Maker

Agentic AI正从辅助工具演变为自主决策系统。70%的供应链从业者对AI效用持乐观态度（Inbound Logistics调查）。多智能体协同系统、物理AI（Physical AI）和数字孪生成为三大技术支柱。全球港航信息化市场2025年达6,406亿元，2026年预计持续快速增长。但数据质量、多Agent治理和可解释性仍是规模化落地的关键瓶颈。

Agentic AI is evolving from an assistive tool to autonomous decision-making systems. 70% of supply chain practitioners are optimistic about AI utility (Inbound Logistics survey). Multi-agent collaborative systems, Physical AI, and digital twins form three technological pillars. The global port & shipping IT market reached about RMB 640.6 billion (≈$90 billion) in 2025, with continued rapid growth expected in 2026. Data quality, multi-agent governance, and explainability remain key bottlenecks for scaling.

趋势二：韧性取代效率成为首要目标

Trend 2: Resilience Replaces Efficiency as Top Goal

Kearney指出，7/10的CEO预期2026年实现10%+收入增长，但供应链成本压力持续——已确认2.3-4%高于基准通胀的成本上升。企业正从"单一来源、零库存、即时交付"的精益模式转向"多源采购、战略库存、灵活响应"的韧性模式。中国26.7万家外贸企业已开拓新的国家和地区市场，进出口值增长22.6%。

Kearney notes 7/10 CEOs expect 10%+ revenue growth in 2026, but cost pressures persist — confirming 2.3-4% above-baseline cost increases. Companies are shifting from lean models (single-source, zero-inventory, JIT) to resilient models (multi-source, strategic inventory, flexible response). 267,000 Chinese foreign trade enterprises have expanded to new markets, with import/export value up 22.6%.

趋势三：绿色供应链从承诺到合规

Trend 3: Green Supply Chains from Pledge to Compliance

绿色供应链正从"附加承诺"走向"硬性合规"：碳报告、强迫劳动尽职调查、产品追溯与范围三排放核算加速嵌入供应链战略。Sphera研究显示，89%的组织计划扩大范围三报告范围，但仅45%信任自身数据。中国绿色出口保持高增——前三季度电动汽车+72.3%、锂电池+36.4%、风电机组+22.4%——供应链竞争力与全球能源转型深度绑定。

Sustainability is no longer a bolt-on: carbon reporting, forced-labor due diligence, product traceability, and Scope 3 accounting are being embedded into supply chain strategy. Sphera research shows 89% of organizations plan to expand Scope 3 reporting, yet only 45% trust their own data. China's green exports stay strong — EVs +72.3%, lithium batteries +36.4%, wind turbines +22.4% in 9M 2026 — binding supply chain competitiveness to the global energy transition.

趋势四：中国供应链"稳定器+创新源"双重角色

Trend 4: China's Dual Role as Stabilizer & Innovator

中国外贸从"量增"向"质优"的结构性转变正在加速。新能源汽车、锂电池、光伏"新三样"之后，以AI、机器人为代表的"新新三样"加速崛起。AI算力硬件进出口5.13万亿元，+56.6%；智能机器人出口超万台，覆盖90余国。对"一带一路"国家进出口12.97万亿元，+14.8%；拉美+16.2%、非洲+19.6%——市场多元化成效显著。

China's foreign trade structural shift from "volume growth" to "quality excellence" is accelerating. After the "New Three" (EVs, lithium batteries, solar), the "Next New Three" (AI, robotics) are rising. AI computing hardware trade reached 5.13 trillion yuan, +56.6%; intelligent robot exports exceeded 10,000 units to 90+ countries. BRI country trade: 12.97 trillion yuan, +14.8%; Latin America +16.2%, Africa +19.6% — market diversification showing significant results.

2026 Q4 Outlook

④ AI规模化落地：电子元件贸易仍是全球贸易最强引擎，Agentic AI与多智能体系统从试点走向核心业务，数据治理与合规风险需持续关注；
⑤ 中国"十五五"规划：《物流网建设实施方案》等政策落地，交通物流一体化、数智化升级将为供应链带来新的结构性红利。

Key focus areas for Q4 2026:
① US-China Trade Trajectory: If rare-earth export controls and the 100% tariff threat take effect, semiconductor, new-energy, and US-bound supply chains face major disruption — watch November negotiations closely;
② Hormuz Situation: Transit remains at low levels, keeping energy and Persian Gulf risk premiums elevated; the "bifurcated" Middle East shipping pattern may persist;
③ Suez Return Pace: If carriers fully return to the Red Sea by year-end, a structural capacity surplus could emerge in H1 2027, pressuring ocean freight rates;
④ AI Scaled Deployment: Electronics trade remains the strongest engine of global trade; Agentic AI and multi-agent systems move from pilots to core operations, with data governance and compliance risks requiring attention;
⑤ China's 15th Five-Year Plan: The Logistics Network Implementation Plan and related policies will bring new structural dividends through transport-logistics integration and digitalization.
