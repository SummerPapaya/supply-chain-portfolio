执行摘要 Executive Summary

2026年上半年，全球供应链在霍尔木兹海峡危机、贸易政策重构与人工智能技术突破三重力量交织下，经历了一场深刻的"压力测试"。中国外贸突破25万亿元历史大关，展现出强大的供应链韧性；全球供应链压力指数（GSCPI）从年初的0.45飙升至4月的1.84，随着停火协议的达成于6月回落至1.25。Agentic AI从概念走向落地，64%的制造商已在推进供应链区域化布局，全球供应链正从"效率优先"转向"韧性为王"的新范式。

In the first half of 2026, global supply chains underwent a profound "stress test" amid the intertwined forces of the Strait of Hormuz crisis, trade policy restructuring, and AI technology breakthroughs. China's foreign trade surpassed the historic 25 trillion yuan mark, demonstrating strong supply chain resilience. The Global Supply Chain Pressure Index (GSCPI) surged from 0.45 at the start of the year to 1.84 in April, before easing to 1.25 by June following ceasefire agreements. Agentic AI moved from concept to deployment, 64% of manufacturers are advancing supply chain regionalization, and global supply chains are shifting from "efficiency-first" to a new paradigm of "resilience reigns."

一、行业综述 I. Industry Overview

全球供应链管理市场规模

Global SCM Market Size

2026年全球供应链管理（SCM）市场规模预计达到约380亿美元（360iResearch），较2025年的354亿美元同比增长约7.4%。到2032年，该市场预计将增长至596亿美元，CAGR为7.72%。云部署解决方案占比超过75%，AI驱动的预测分析和自动化正成为核心增长引擎。

The global Supply Chain Management (SCM) market is estimated to reach approximately $38 billion in 2026 (360iResearch), up 7.4% from $35.4 billion in 2025. The market is projected to reach $59.6 billion by 2032 at a CAGR of 7.72%. Cloud-based solutions account for over 75% of deployments, with AI-driven predictive analytics and automation serving as core growth engines.

全球贸易总量

Global Trade Volume

WTO数据显示，2026年Q1全球商品贸易量环比增长1.9%，同比增长3.2%。WTO货物贸易晴雨表指数报101.7（1月为102.3），仍高于100基准线，表明贸易量仍高于趋势水平。AI相关电子产品贸易同比增长超40%，成为抵消中东冲突负面影响的关键力量。

WTO data shows Q1 2026 global merchandise trade volume grew 1.9% QoQ and 3.2% YoY. The WTO Goods Trade Barometer stands at 101.7 (down from 102.3 in January), still above the 100 baseline, indicating trade volumes remain above trend. AI-related electronics trade surged over 40% YoY, serving as a key counterweight to Middle East conflict disruptions.

中国外贸：历史性突破

2026年上半年，中国货物贸易进出口总值达25.47万亿元，首次突破25万亿元关口，同比增长16.9%。出口已连续11个季度保持正向增长，全年增速稳定超过10%。进口份额从全球7.9%提升至约10%，连续17年稳居全球第二大进口市场。

中国物流运行：稳中有进

上半年社会物流总额181.1万亿元，同比增长5.1%，高于GDP增速0.4个百分点。物流业总收入7.2万亿元，增长4.7%。社会物流总费用与GDP比率为13.9%，较上年同期回落0.1个百分点。铁路货运发送量20.15亿吨，增长1.8%；快递业务量1003.8亿件，增长5%。

Global Trade Resilience

Despite the Strait of Hormuz crisis, global merchandise trade demonstrated remarkable resilience. The WTO Goods Trade Barometer of 101.7 suggests continued above-trend growth. However, UNCTAD warns that the headline 12.5% H1 trade volume expansion is significantly inflated by shipping cost pass-through from the Middle East disruptions.

Regional Divergence

Asia led Q1 2026 trade growth with exports +12.9% and imports +14.6% YoY, driven by AI-sector demand. North America showed mixed performance with exports +7.0% but imports -10.7% YoY. The Middle East was hardest hit, with Q1 exports -9.7% and imports -11.9%, with Q2 declines expected to be even steeper.

二、行业动态 II. Industry Dynamics

🔴 核心事件：霍尔木兹海峡危机重塑全球航运格局

🔴 Key Event: Strait of Hormuz Crisis Reshapes Global Shipping

2月28日美以对伊朗发动军事打击以来，霍尔木兹海峡——承载全球约1/4海运石油贸易和1/5液化天然气贸易的关键咽喉——通航量断崖式下跌。3月前两周仅77艘船只通过（同期正常水平为1,200+艘）。马士基、MSC、达飞、赫伯罗特等航运巨头纷纷宣布暂停经海峡航线，大规模绕行好望角。航程增加3,500-4,000海里、运输时间延长10-14天、相关航线运费上涨15-60%。战争保险保费从0.25%飙升至3%-10%。6月17日美伊达成停火协议后，海峡通航量恢复至正常水平的约60%，但7月底局势再度恶化。

Since the US-Israel military strikes on Iran on February 28, the Strait of Hormuz — carrying approximately 1/4 of global seaborne oil trade and 1/5 of LNG trade — saw transit volumes collapse. Only 77 vessels passed in the first two weeks of March (vs. 1,200+ normally). Shipping giants Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM, and Hapag-Lloyd suspended Hormuz routes, rerouting via the Cape of Good Hope. Voyages extended by 3,500-4,000 nautical miles / 10-14 days, with freight rates rising 15-60%. War risk insurance surged from 0.25% to 3-10% of hull value. Following the June 17 ceasefire, transit recovered to ~60% of normal, but conditions deteriorated again in late July.

主要贸易政策变动

Major Trade Policy Changes

  • 美国最高法院关税裁决（1月）：限制总统单方面大幅调整关税权力。Kearney报告指出平均实际关税率已上升30%。 US Supreme Court Tariff Ruling (Jan): Limits presidential power to unilaterally adjust tariffs. Kearney reports applied tariff rates have risen 30% on average.
  • USMCA续期受阻（6月）：美国拒绝以现有形式续期16年，改为年度审查机制。60%受调查企业尚未采取应对行动。 USMCA Renewal Blocked (Jun): US declines 16-year renewal; shifts to annual reviews. 60% of surveyed firms have taken no preparatory action.
  • 欧盟关税豁免取消（7月生效）：取消150欧元以下包裹关税豁免，每件加征3欧元，直接影响跨境电商。 EU De Minimis Removal (effective July): Eliminates duty exemption for packages under €150; €3 surcharge per item, directly impacting cross-border e-commerce.
  • Kearney Navigator预警：供应链成本将比基准通胀高出2.3%-4%。地缘政治风险上升34%，全球库存水平上升14%，关键矿产出口下降1/3。 Kearney Navigator Alert: Supply chain costs to run 2.3-4% above baseline inflation. Geopolitical risk +34%, global inventory +14%, critical mineral exports -33% YoY.

四、热门议题 IV. Hot Topics

🤖 Agentic AI：供应链的新"数字员工"

🤖 Agentic AI: Supply Chain's New "Digital Workforce"

2026年被业界称为供应链AI的"iPhone时刻"。Agentic AI（代理型人工智能）从概念验证走向规模化商用，AI从"回答问题"进化到"自主执行"。Gartner预测，到2026年底40%的企业应用将集成专属AI代理。壹沓科技、全球捷运等企业已在物流截单、合同审核、运价解析等场景实现5倍效率提升。青岛港自研"星港大模型"实现安全隐患全闭环管控，监管覆盖率提升至98%以上。全球港航信息化市场规模达6,406亿元，+7.45%。

2026 is called the "iPhone moment" for supply chain AI. Agentic AI has moved from proof-of-concept to commercial deployment, evolving from "answering questions" to "autonomous execution." Gartner predicts 40% of enterprise applications will integrate dedicated AI agents by end-2026. Companies like Yita Technology are delivering 5x efficiency improvements in document processing, contract review, and rate parsing. Port of Qingdao's AI model achieves 98%+ safety coverage. Global port & shipping IT market reaches RMB 640.6 billion, +7.45%.

🌍 供应链区域化：从"去风险"到"再布局"

🌍 Supply Chain Regionalization: From De-risking to Re-design

64%的制造商已推进或正在推进生产区域化（Alpega调查）。中国58%的供应链高管计划增加供应商数量，43%预期贸易壁垒将进一步增加。北美买家的采购集中度显著下降，印度、越南等市场承接了大量订单转移。Prologis调查显示：66%的企业计划扩大在墨西哥的布局，62%计划扩大在美国的布局。然而，近岸/回岸仍占总体采购的较小份额——成本结构、品类适配和供应商深度仍是主要制约。

64% of manufacturers have regionalized or are regionalizing production (Alpega survey). 58% of Chinese supply chain executives plan to increase supplier counts; 43% expect further trade barrier increases. North American buyers' sourcing concentration has materially decreased, with India, Vietnam absorbing significant order shifts. Prologis survey: 66% plan to expand in Mexico, 62% in the US. However, nearshoring/reshoring remains a small share of total sourcing — cost structures, category fit, and supplier depth remain key constraints.

🛢️ 能源与航运：霍尔木兹的长尾效应

🛢️ Energy & Shipping: Hormuz Long-Tail Effects

霍尔木兹海峡危机虽在6月因停火协议出现转机，但其影响远未结束。航线改道已成为新的标准——绕行好望角使单程增加3,500-4,000海里。德班、巴生港、科伦坡等替代中转枢纽持续拥堵。能源贸易的物理路线和合同期限正在发生结构性改变：主要进口方加速采购来源多元化，增加长期协议和战略储备。中欧班列开行量逆势增长20%，陆路通道战略价值凸显。

The Hormuz crisis, though eased by the June ceasefire, has lasting effects. Route diversions have become the new norm — Cape of Good Hope detours add 3,500-4,000 nautical miles. Alternative hubs (Durban, Port Klang, Colombo) remain congested. Energy trade routes and contract structures are undergoing structural change: major importers accelerate source diversification, longer-term agreements, and strategic reserves. China-Europe Railway Express volume grew 20%, highlighting the strategic value of land corridors.

🔄 供应链韧性指数：从"事后救火"到"事前预判"

🔄 Supply Chain Resilience: From Reactive to Predictive

第四届链博会发布的《全球供应链促进报告2026》揭示了关键洞察：2018-2025年间，全球供应链促进指数年均增长16.07%，创新指数增长14.4%，而韧性指数仅增长3.23%。韧性指数已处于体系"枢纽"地位，与促进、连接、创新之间存在显著因果关联。采用AI自主决策系统的企业响应中断速度提升62%、恢复速度加快60%（Accenture）。"韧性为王"正在成为新的行业共识。

The 4th CISCE released the Global Supply Chain Promotion Report 2026, revealing that from 2018-2025, the promotion index grew at 16.07% annually, innovation at 14.4%, but resilience at just 3.23% — now in a pivotal "hub" position. Companies adopting autonomous AI-driven supply chains respond to disruptions 62% faster and recover 60% quicker (Accenture). "Resilience reigns" is becoming the new industry consensus.

趋势一：AI从工具到决策主体

Trend 1: AI from Tool to Decision-Maker

Agentic AI正从辅助工具演变为自主决策系统。70%的供应链从业者对AI效用持乐观态度（Inbound Logistics调查）。多智能体协同系统、物理AI（Physical AI）和数字孪生成为三大技术支柱。全球港航信息化市场2025年达6,406亿元，2026年预计持续快速增长。但数据质量、多Agent治理和可解释性仍是规模化落地的关键瓶颈。

Agentic AI is evolving from an assistive tool to autonomous decision-making systems. 70% of supply chain practitioners are optimistic about AI utility (Inbound Logistics survey). Multi-agent collaborative systems, Physical AI, and digital twins form three technological pillars. The global port & shipping IT market reached ~$900 billion in 2025, with continued rapid growth expected in 2026. Data quality, multi-agent governance, and explainability remain key bottlenecks for scaling.

趋势二：韧性取代效率成为首要目标

Trend 2: Resilience Replaces Efficiency as Top Goal

Kearney指出，7/10的CEO预期2026年实现10%+收入增长，但供应链成本压力持续——已确认2.3-4%高于基准通胀的成本上升。企业正从"单一来源、零库存、即时交付"的精益模式转向"多源采购、战略库存、灵活响应"的韧性模式。中国26.7万家外贸企业已开拓新的国家和地区市场，进出口值增长22.6%。

Kearney notes 7/10 CEOs expect 10%+ revenue growth in 2026, but cost pressures persist — confirming 2.3-4% above-baseline cost increases. Companies are shifting from lean models (single-source, zero-inventory, JIT) to resilient models (multi-source, strategic inventory, flexible response). 267,000 Chinese foreign trade enterprises have expanded to new markets, with import/export value up 22.6%.

China's foreign trade structural shift from "volume growth" to "quality excellence" is accelerating. After the "New Three" (EVs, lithium batteries, solar), the "Next New Three" (AI, robotics) are rising. AI computing hardware trade reached 5.13 trillion yuan, +56.6%; intelligent robot exports exceeded 10,000 units to 90+ countries. BRI country trade: 12.97 trillion yuan, +14.8%; Latin America +16.2%, Africa +19.6% — market diversification showing significant results.
